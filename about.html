One building.
One family. Twelve years.

Huntington Collision Center has been at 99 E. Jericho Turnpike since 2013. The same people who write your estimate are the ones who pull the frame, spray the panel and hand you the keys.

Nothing here gets
sent somewhere else.

Plenty of shops on Long Island take your car, then sublet the paint to one place and the mechanical to another. You never find out who actually touched it. We built this shop the other way around: frame bench, prep bays, sealed paint booth and the detail room are all under this roof.

It also means when something is not right, there is one phone number to call and one person who answers for it.

How we work,
every single time.

Four things we do not bend on, whether the car is a work truck or an AMG.

Factory procedure, not shortcuts

Structural work follows the manufacturer's repair procedure. If a panel is meant to be replaced rather than pulled, we replace it, and the estimate says so in writing.

You see the car mid-job

Progress photos as the work moves, not a two-week silence followed by an invoice. If the timeline slips, you hear it from us first.

Lifetime workmanship warranty

For as long as you own the vehicle. If our work fails, we fix it. That covers the labour and the finish, not the wear of a normal life on Long Island roads.

Every car leaves detailed

Washed, vacuumed and wiped down before pickup, at no charge, on a $400 bumper job or a $14,000 rebuild. It is the last thing you see, so it matters.
