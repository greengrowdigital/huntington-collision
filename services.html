Two things worth knowing before you call anyone.

You pick the shop, not the insurer

In New York your carrier can recommend a shop but cannot require one. If they steer you somewhere, you can say no and still be covered.

The first estimate is rarely the last

Hidden damage shows up once the panel comes off. That is what a supplement is for, and writing it is our job, not yours.
